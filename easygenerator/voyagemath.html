Case study 1 · VoyageMath

Driving cross-product adoption: combining funnel data and qualitative research to grow VoyageMath adoption among middle school math teachers on Wayground from 5% to 13%

(formerly Quizizz) is a supplemental curriculum platform that teachers in 90% of US schools use for instruction, reviews and assessments.

is Wayground's sister platform for math practice, with an adaptive question bank and step-by-step guidance. It caters to middle school math teachers in the US.

The problem

Only 5% of middle school math teachers on Wayground were also active on VoyageMath, against a 20% target for the quarter, with drop-offs at every stage of the funnel.

The research

A study in five parts, each building on the last: from discovery to design, usability testing and shipping.

ObjectiveWhat I foundWhat we shipped
Find where VoyageMath fits in a teacher's lessons and units, to know when and where to show it10 semi-structured interviews
  • Teachers used it after a lesson, to reinforce skills
  • And after a unit review, to reteach and give struggling students guided practice
VoyageMath practice for each skill, inside Wayground units
Understand teachers' Jobs To Be Done after a unit review, to position VoyageMath around them8 semi-structured interviews
  • Teachers check which students are struggling most, and on which standards and questions
  • Then they reteach, or assign extra practice to those students
Struggling students and relevant activities pre-selected when assigning practice
Use the previous phase's findings to prototype a Wayground post-review email that gets teachers to open it, sign up for VoyageMath and assign practicePrototyping, iterative concept and usability testing with 12 teachers
  • Teachers wanted both the questions and the standards students struggled with
  • They liked activities and students pre-selected on VoyageMath at sign-up
  • They clicked when it read like a Wayground report, not an ad
Post-unit-review email that surfaces VoyageMath
Redesign the Wayground reports page to surface VoyageMath after a unit reviewPrototyping and usability testing with 6 teachers
  • Teachers wanted struggling students pre-selected with the practice sets
  • Picking them by hand was too much work
New Wayground post-review reports page that cross-promotes VoyageMath
Find out why teachers who clicked through abandoned VoyageMath onboarding before signing upUsability testing of the onboarding with 8 teachers new to VoyageMath
  • It was unclear if VoyageMath fit their grade, curriculum and standards
  • The adaptive questions and step-by-step guidance weren't visible
  • Teachers didn't know their Wayground classes carry straight over
Rebuilt VoyageMath onboarding

Impact

5% → 13%middle school math teachers on Wayground who were also active on VoyageMath

We also saw improvement at every stage of the overall funnel.

Reflection: what moved each stage of the funnel

The June numbers come from the last weeks before summer break, when teachers run a lot of reviews and tests. Late September is the start of the school year, with far fewer reviews, so June had more natural demand for VoyageMath. Even so, every stage improved.

StageChangeWhat drove it
Clicked4,005 → ~6,590 (+65%)Surfacing VoyageMath at the right time, in the right places: the post-review email, the reports page on Wayground and Wayground units
Signed up~19% → ~38% of clicksThe new positioning and the rebuilt onboarding made the value clear, so twice as many teachers finished signing up
