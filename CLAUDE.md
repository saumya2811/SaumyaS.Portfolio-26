# CLAUDE.md

Saumya Srivastava's UX research portfolio, a static multi-page site published with GitHub Pages. No build step.

- `index.html`: home page. First screen (`.home`, ~82vh): photo, "Hello, I'm Saumya." and a short personal about on the left; link list on the right (`.hls` > `.grp` > `.hl`): Work ↓ (scrolls to `#work`) and Resume, then Email (connect@saumyasri.in) and LinkedIn. "Book a 1-1" (`#book-1-1`) links to her Calendly (https://calendly.com/connect-saumyasri/30min). "Outside of work" is planned but not added yet. An "Education" link (↓, scrolls to `#education`) sits right after Work. Below, `#work` holds a COPY of the `work.html` case study sections and end note, so edit titles/cards in both files. Footer Email links on index, product and work pages go to connect@saumyasri.in
- `#education` on `index.html` (after `#work`, a full-width soft sand band, #FAF6EE, with 120px space above it): the MS by Research (thesis title linked to `thesis.pdf`, the full thesis hosted on the site, a "Thesis deck" link to `thesis-presentation.pdf`, a one-paragraph summary, ICMPC'23 abstracts) with the convocation photo `img/convocation-2026.jpg` beside it; the heading reads "B.Tech in CS + MS by Research…" (no separate B.Tech line). The `.end-note` closing line sits at the end of `#work`, right before Education
- `product/index.html` (live at saumyasri.in/product/): product-role front page, a full copy of `index.html` with every relative link prefixed `../` (same photo, link list, `#work` and `#education`) with only the title, the two bio paragraphs, the Resume link (`Saumya-Srivastava-Product-Resume.pdf`, "Product & Growth") and the sessionStorage value (`/product/`) changed; when editing work/education on `index.html`, mirror the change here too. Not linked from the nav, shared directly with PM applications. `product.html` is now only a redirect to `/product/` for old links. `work.html`'s back link returns to whichever front page the visitor came from (sessionStorage key `front`)
- `work.html`: the five case studies, grouped by SrotaBio, Wayground and Rush (by Hike). Each company heading has a small grey domain label (`.dom`: healthcare / edtech / gaming). The Wayground dropdown ends with a playtests point and a row of three photos (`.pt-row`; `img/wg-playtest-school.jpg`, `img/wg-playtest.jpg`, `img/wg-playtest-room.jpg`; children's faces blurred, never add identifiable children). Each company section is two columns (`.co-grid`): case study cards on the left (`.co-cards`), the dropdown on the right ("What else I've been up to at SrotaBio", "More from my time at Wayground/Hike") (`.co-more`); stacks on narrow screens. On wider screens an opened dropdown shows as a full-width panel (white card with shadow, × button; closes on outside click or Esc; one open at a time). A small script at the end of the page adds bottom margin to its `.co-grid` so the panel pushes the next section down instead of covering it
- `srotabio.html`, `voyagemath.html`, `accommodations.html`, `hike-revenue.html`, `hike-immersion.html`: case study pages; their back links go to the Work section on the home page (`index.html#work`), or to `/product/#work` if the visitor came in through the product page (sessionStorage key `front`), with a "Next" link to the following case study
- `work.html` ends with a plain, low-key line (`.end-note`): "These are just the highlights. Curious about my process? Let's talk: drop me an email or book a 1-1." (email → connect@saumyasri.in, 1-1 → Calendly). The case study pages no longer have their own contact note
- `CNAME`: custom domain (saumyasri.in) for GitHub Pages; don't remove it
- `img/`: images, including `img/tool/` screenshots for the SrotaBio carousel
