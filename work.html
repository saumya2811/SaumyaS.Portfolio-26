More about my work at Wayground

Researchers at Wayground were each dedicated to a product pod. I was the dedicated researcher on the Accommodations and VoyageMath pods.

I was part of a closely knit, cross-functional team with product, design, analytics, content and sales, and worked directly with senior leadership. My work balanced evaluative research on adoption, retention and customer ratings with building a foundational understanding of teachers and their worlds to shape product roadmaps.

Beyond research projects, and with limited research resources, I helped shape the overall research culture at Wayground by:

  • Coaching PMs, designers and content teams on pods without a dedicated researcher to set up their own research processes
  • Starting a research newsletter, online and in print, to share findings across the company
  • Building recruitment practices and long-term relationships with hard-to-reach users like SPED teachers, as part of the Accommodations pod
